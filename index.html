QUESTIONS

Asked and answered.

What does FreeCoffee actually show?

One compact ad in the Mac status area, from categories you choose. It never reads screen contents, files, browser history, window titles, keystrokes, or screenshots.

How much do I earn?

You keep 65% of the gross winning media price for every verified minute your Mac serves. That rises to 70% once platform revenue passes $50k a month. A verified click adds a $0.05 bonus, capped at 5 a day.

Is there a minimum before I get paid?

Yes — $2, and the payout rail holds a new balance for 24 hours. Those limits exist to keep the ledger honest while the platform is small, and they are published rather than discovered.

What do advertisers pay for?

Verified airtime on eligible, non-interrupted display, plus clicks. Duplicate and invalid traffic is filtered out and never billed. Advertisers see aggregate counts and percentages — never device identifiers.

Does an ad run before anyone reviews it?

Campaigns submitted through the open queue are reviewed by a human before they serve. A winning slot bid enters the serving list directly, so bid on creatives you would stand behind publicly.

Which platforms are supported?

The native Mac app is the full experience. The web dashboard and installable PWA are supported surfaces too, and delivery is reported per surface so you can see where your minutes came from.
