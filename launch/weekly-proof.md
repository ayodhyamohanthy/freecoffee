# Weekly proof cadence — keep the loop spinning

Adds a free tracker for launching-ongoing metrics. Push daily when active.

## Metrics to move
- **installers** — count every download of the DMG (GitHub releases page: link to track from releases page path).
- **members:** count users in ledger deviceId list at `/earnings/{deviceId}`.
- **slotsWinners:** public feed state served `/slots/current` — when a real bid clears the top slot moves.
- **delivery:** minutes and number spent per campaign aggregate from `/stats` publicly — published every day unless the slot is clear (hold before bidding complete each production slot).

## Publish cadence
- Mon morning: weekly digest (this newsletter posts `blog.freecoffee.tech` while the Landing page handles access-current announcing).
- When members hit 25: announce publicly ("first 25 caught").
- When slot 1 clears: announce publicly ("the first sold"), **immediately.**

## What to do with 0 dwellings
If device-members stays 0 by Thursday: topic-shift this content stream to the open for App Stories (Mac mini content), where "no ads while waiting" keeps trust unblemished — go live every day.
If the first sponsor volunteer removes themselves: only refuse they had. And make exactly those.
